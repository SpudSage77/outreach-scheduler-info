Privacy Policy

Outreach Scheduler · Last updated November 1, 2026

Outreach Scheduler is a personal-use desktop application operated by its owner. It helps the owner prepare, schedule, and send email outreach using their own Gmail account.

Google account access

The application requests the Gmail send permission (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send) to send messages authorized or scheduled by the owner. It does not require access to read the contents of the owner's Gmail inbox for its core sending function.

Information processed

The application processes outreach contact information, email addresses, message drafts, sending schedules, and any attachments the owner selects. It may retain local records of sent messages, delivery attempts, and Gmail message identifiers to support scheduling and history.

Storage and sharing

Application data and Gmail authorization credentials are stored in the owner's local application environment. To send an email, the application transmits the message and selected attachments to Google's Gmail API; Gmail then processes and delivers the email to its recipients. The application is not a public service and does not sell Google user data.

Access and deletion

The owner controls the local application and its stored outreach records. Google account access can be revoked through the Google Account permissions page. Local application records and credentials can be removed by the owner from their device.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the application's features or data handling change.
